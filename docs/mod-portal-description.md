# Discord Rich Presence

Shows your save, planet, current research, and playtime on your Discord profile
while you play. Choose exactly what's shown, per player, from *Settings → Mods*.
Detects popular overhauls (Krastorio2, Space Age, pyanodons, bob's & angel's,
Space Exploration) automatically.

Needs the free, open-source companion app for Windows to talk to Discord:
**[github.com/enrik-0/factorio-discord-rich-presence](https://github.com/enrik-0/factorio-discord-rich-presence)**

Requires Factorio **2.0** or later.
